Digital Transformation – Platinum Support & Care

Taking operations from paper to digital with Access, Power BI, and mobile logging for carers.

  • Access
  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • Mobile Forms
  • Governance

Project Overview

The organisation relied entirely on paper for rotas, visit logs, and training. I designed a central Microsoft Access database, connected it to Power BI for interactive KPIs, and introduced a secure mobile logging workflow so carers could record visits on the go. I led training and adoption for non-technical staff.

  • Relational Access schema (Clients, Staff, Visits, Training)
  • Power Query ETL and scheduled refresh to Power BI
  • Role-based views and RLS for managers/carers
  • Staff onboarding materials and support clinics
← Back to Analytics

Problem

  • Manual, paper-based reporting (slow and error-prone)
  • Fragmented records; no real-time visibility
  • Compliance tracking difficult and reactive

Solution

  • Access data model, forms and validation rules
  • Power BI KPIs with DAX, RLS, and drill-through
  • Mobile forms for carers (secure auth; minimal taps)
  • Data governance and process documentation

Impact

  • Weekly reports → generated in minutes
  • Better data accuracy and auditability
  • Live KPI tracking for visits & training
  • Improved resource planning and service quality
