Project Overview

As CRM Coordinator, I supported a busy recruitment database across Northern Ireland. I built Access/Excel queries to extract and clean records, designed monthly KPI dashboards, ran data audits, and trained users to standardise data entry — enabling faster decisions and better pipeline visibility.

  • Placement funnel (applications → interviews → placements)
  • Client engagement and job fill rates
  • Validation checks & exception reports
  • User training and quick-reference guides

Problem

  • Inconsistent CRM data entry across teams
  • Manual reporting slowed weekly ops reviews
  • Limited visibility into conversion bottlenecks

Solution

  • Access/Excel queries for clean extracts
  • Pivot-based KPI dashboards (Power Pivot)
  • Standardised data dictionaries & picklists
  • Staff training and support clinics

Impact

  • Faster KPI cycles; reliable month-end packs
  • Better pipeline visibility for account leads
  • Higher data integrity → fewer reworks
