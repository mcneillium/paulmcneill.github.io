Privacy Policy

Voice — AI-Powered AAC App
Last updated: 24 March 2026

1. Introduction

Paul Martin McNeill ("we", "us", or "our") built Voice as a free/commercial application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Voice app ("Service").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account or profile details (e.g. name, email) if you choose to create an account.
  • Text, symbols, or phrases you enter while using the AAC features.
  • Caregiver dashboard settings and preferences.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers).
  • Usage data (features used, session duration, crash logs).

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise geolocation data.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone unless you explicitly grant permission for specific features (e.g. speech output).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Service's core AAC and predictive-text features.
  • To personalise communication boards and suggestions.
  • To generate anonymised, aggregated analytics that help us improve the app.
  • To respond to support requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers – trusted third parties that help us operate the Service (e.g. cloud hosting, analytics). These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8).

6. Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Voice may be used by or on behalf of children under the supervision of a parent or caregiver. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Third-Party Services

The Service may contain links to or integrate with third-party services (e.g. Google Play Services). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above.
