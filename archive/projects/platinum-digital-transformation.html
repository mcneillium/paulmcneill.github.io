Digital Transformation – Platinum Support & Care

From paper-based operations to a secure, data-driven platform with Access, Power BI, and mobile logging for carers.

Project Overview

I led the end-to-end digitisation of a care provider’s information management. The organisation relied entirely on paper for rotas, visit logs, and training records. I designed a central Microsoft Access database, connected it to Power BI for interactive KPIs, and introduced a secure mobile logging workflow so carers could record visits on the go.

  • Central data model in Access (clients, staff, visits, training)
  • Power Query ETL; scheduled refresh to Power BI reports
  • Role-based views for managers vs. carers
  • Training & adoption plan for non-technical users
← Back to Analytics

The Problem

Paper workflows caused delays, errors, and limited visibility:

  • Time-consuming manual reporting
  • Duplicate/fragmented records
  • No real-time view of visits or compliance

The Solution

  • Relational Access schema; forms & validation rules
  • Power BI dashboards (RLS, DAX measures, drill-through)
  • Mobile forms for carers (secure auth, minimal taps)
  • Data governance & user training materials

The Impact

  • Reporting time reduced (weekly → minutes)
  • Higher data accuracy and auditability
  • Live KPI tracking (visits, response times, training)
  • Better resource planning and service quality
