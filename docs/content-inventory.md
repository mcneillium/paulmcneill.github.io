# Content inventory

Every page on the site, what it's for, and what it needs. Status legend:

- **Strong** — content is concrete, evidence-backed, ready for visitors.
- **OK** — fine, could be tightened.
- **Placeholder** — visible to users but flagged TODO.
- **Stub** — minimal content; needs writing.
