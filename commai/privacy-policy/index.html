CommAI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 March 2026

CommAI is an Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) application designed to help non-verbal and minimally verbal individuals express themselves. This privacy policy explains what information CommAI collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

CommAI is developed by Paul Martin McNeill ("we", "us", "our").

1. Information We Collect

Account & Authentication Data

When you create an account, CommAI collects your email address and a display name. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (provided by Google). We do not store your password; it is managed entirely by Firebase.

AAC Usage & Personalisation Data

To provide predictive text suggestions, personalised vocabulary, and caregiver insights, CommAI may store:

  • Phrases and words you select or type
  • Custom vocabulary boards and pictogram configurations
  • UI preferences such as grid size, theme, and font settings
  • Session-level usage metrics (e.g. phrases used per session, time-to-utter)

This data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and is associated with your user account. Where possible, usage metrics are aggregated and do not contain the raw text of your communications.

Image Captioning & AI-Assisted Features

CommAI includes a camera-based image captioning feature that uses AI to describe scenes and objects, helping users build communication around their environment. When you use this feature:

  • Images are processed by a third-party AI service to generate captions
  • Images are sent securely over an encrypted connection and are not retained by the AI provider beyond the time needed to produce a caption
  • Generated captions may be stored locally on your device or in your account for use within the AAC interface

Device & Technical Data

CommAI may collect basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version for the purpose of diagnosing issues and improving compatibility. This data is not used to identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the AAC communication experience
  • To power predictive text and personalised vocabulary suggestions
  • To generate caregiver dashboard insights (session summaries, usage trends)
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app stability

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core AAC functionality of CommAI.

3. Third-Party Services

CommAI uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, real-time database, and cloud storage. Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • AI / Machine Learning services — used for image captioning and predictive text features. Data sent to these services is limited to what is necessary for the specific feature and is transmitted over encrypted connections.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

4. Data Sharing

Your personal data is not shared with third parties except as described above (Firebase and AI services) for the sole purpose of operating CommAI. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any unrelated services.

Caregiver dashboard data is only accessible to caregiver accounts that have been explicitly linked to a user's profile by an authorised administrator.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data and AAC usage data are retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove your data from our systems within 30 days.

You can also export your data from within the app before requesting deletion.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Firebase security rules restrict data access to authorised users only
  • Authentication tokens are managed securely by Firebase Authentication
  • Access to the database and admin tools is restricted to authorised personnel

While no system can guarantee absolute security, we are committed to following industry best practices to protect your data.

7. Children's Privacy

CommAI may be used by or on behalf of children as part of their communication support. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the involvement of a parent, guardian, or authorised caregiver. All accounts for minors should be created and managed by a responsible adult.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of CommAI after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
