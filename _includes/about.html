About

Building from the island of Ireland

I’m Paul, an AI engineer, web developer and data analyst based on the island of Ireland. I build LLM-powered tools, web platforms and analytics for healthcare, accessibility and day-to-day operations.

My recent work includes NHSCT-aligned digital transformation, with Power BI reporting for care operations and compliance, and AI accessibility tools such as a predictive AAC app for non-verbal users.

I hold a master’s in Artificial Intelligence from Queen’s University Belfast and a First-Class BSc (Hons) in Computing from The Open University. I’ve also completed the Open University’s postgraduate M816 Data Management module.
