About Me

AI Technologist • Data Analyst • Creative Director

I’m Paul Martin McNeill — a technologist passionate about applying artificial intelligence, data analytics, and software engineering to create inclusive and intelligent digital systems. My focus lies at the intersection of human-centred design and emerging technology, building tools that empower people and improve everyday life.

My most recent work, the AAC App, represents a significant step in assistive communication technology (AAC). This cross-platform application enables individuals with speech and language difficulties to communicate through pictogram-based interfaces enhanced by on-device AI predictions. Built with React Native, TensorFlow.js, and Firebase, the app provides real-time caregiver dashboards, offline support, and data-driven insights — demonstrating how ethical AI can enhance accessibility, autonomy, and care.

The project integrates machine learning, cloud architecture, and accessibility engineering — showcasing skills in Python, JavaScript, TensorFlow, Power BI, and SQL. It also embodies my commitment to ethical computing: user data privacy (GDPR), WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, and transparent AI interaction were designed into every component. The system is being developed as an open-source framework to make assistive technology more affordable and inclusive.

I’ve delivered transformative data analytics solutions for organisations such as Platinum Support & Care Services, leading a digital transformation from paper-based reporting to real-time performance intelligence. Using Microsoft Access, Power BI, and the Power Platform, I centralised data flows, automated reporting, and built KPI dashboards that improved operational efficiency and strategic visibility.

Earlier in my career, as Creative Director at Paramount Perceptions, I combined technical precision with visual design, delivering web platforms that united brand identity with functionality. This role refined my ability to lead multi-disciplinary teams, manage client expectations, and deliver innovative solutions that balance form and function — skills that now underpin my AI and data-driven development projects.

I hold multiple professional certifications, including Google Cloud Professional Machine Learning Engineer and DeepLearning.AI TensorFlow Developer credentials, supported by a First-Class BSc (Hons) in Computing and IT from the Open University. My training covers data engineering, cloud deployment, and full-stack AI integration, providing a solid foundation for advanced research and intelligent system design.

I actively collaborate with developers, healthcare professionals, and academics to advance technology that serves society — from predictive analytics to inclusive AI. My guiding philosophy is simple: technology should adapt to people, not the other way around. Whether through data-driven insight or AI-powered communication tools, my goal is to build systems that improve lives with empathy, intelligence, and precision.
