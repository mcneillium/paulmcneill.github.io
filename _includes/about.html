About

Building from the island of Ireland

I’m Paul, an AI engineer, web developer and data analyst based on the island of Ireland. I build machine learning forecasting tools, web platforms and analytics for water infrastructure, healthcare, accessibility and day-to-day operations.

My current work is a leakage forecasting and planning platform for a UK water utility, covering more than 1,250 district metered areas. Alongside it I run reproducible research on public data, and earlier work includes an AI-enhanced care-management app built on synthetic data and AI accessibility tools such as a predictive AAC app for non-verbal users.

I’m studying for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Queen’s University Belfast (due 2027) and hold a First-Class BSc (Hons) in Computer Science from The Open University. I’ve also completed the Open University’s postgraduate M816 Data Management module.
