Cancer drug response

What did the three clusters pick up?

In a study of 924 cell lines, I looked more closely at the 128 with an altered PTEN gene. Three groups kept reappearing when I repeated the analysis.

All 20 lines in the third group came from blood or lymphoid tissue. After accounting for tissue and sequencing centre, I found no convincing subgroup with this method. Stable clusters alone gave me little reason to interpret them as drug-response groups.
