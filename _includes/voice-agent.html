Ask about Paul’s work, by voice

A live AI voice assistant that answers questions about Paul’s projects, research and the Voice app, from a fact sheet taken from this site. It is not Paul, and it says so. You can also type instead of speaking.

  • Runs on ElevenLabs. What you say is sent to ElevenLabs to be transcribed and answered; nothing loads from ElevenLabs until you press the button below.
  • Audio is not kept. The text transcript is kept for 30 days so Paul can check the answers, then deleted.
  • Conversations are limited to four minutes, and the assistant has a small daily allowance, so it may sometimes be unavailable.
