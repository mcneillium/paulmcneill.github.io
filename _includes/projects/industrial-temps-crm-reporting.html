Project Overview

As CRM Coordinator, I supported a busy recruitment database, focusing on data quality, repeatable reporting, and staff adoption. I built Access/Excel queries to extract and clean records, designed monthly KPI dashboards, and trained users to standardise data entry for better insights and faster decision-making.

  • Placement funnel metrics (applications → interviews → placements)
  • Client engagement and job fill rates
  • Data validation checks; exception reports
  • User training & quick-reference guides
← Back to Analytics

The Problem

  • Inconsistent CRM data entry across teams
  • Manual reporting slowed weekly ops reviews
  • Limited visibility on conversion bottlenecks

The Solution

  • Access/Excel queries for clean extracts
  • Pivot-based KPI dashboards (Power Pivot)
  • Standardised data dictionaries & picklists
  • Staff training and support clinics

The Impact

  • Faster KPI cycles; reliable month-end packs
  • Better pipeline visibility for account leads
  • Higher data integrity → fewer reworks
